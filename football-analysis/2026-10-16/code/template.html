Fixture Probability Ledger

All 111 uploaded fixtures across nine competitions: Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Bundesliga, Süper Lig, and the Champions, Europa and Conference League matchdays. Every team's full current season was reviewed, every opponent was rated on one cross-league scale, and every estimate comes from a single scoreline distribution per match.

Overall master ranking

The 40 highest final probabilities across all five outcome types. Forty is a display length, not a threshold: every other outcome is in the matrix and the per-market lists. Selections from the same fixture are correlated and marked; nothing here is combined into an accumulator.

SECTION 9

Detailed evidence for the master selections

One card per master-ranking selection, in rank order. The counterargument and final assessment were written by the analyst from the data shown; the other fields are computed.

SECTION 10

Matches with weak or conflicting evidence

A fixture appears here if its best outcome is below 55%, or if two or more evidence problems apply. It is listed under its main problem, and every problem is shown.

APPENDIX

Fixture dossiers

The full research file for each match: both teams' current-season splits (all, home, away, last 10, last 5, league, UEFA, cup), every match played this season with halftime score, xG, shots, shots on target, big chances and opponent strength, comparable-opponent results, absentees weighted by minutes played, rest days, coaching changes, weather and head-to-head.

METHOD

Method, validation and sources

Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees, and cover 90 minutes plus stoppage time only. The model and backtest were built for this report; calibration is shown in Section 1. No outcome here is certain, and correlated selections should not be treated as independent.
