Complete probability matrix

Every uploaded fixture. Final estimates are a single Dixon-Coles scoreline distribution fitted to a blend of 25% independent model and 75% de-vigged bookmaker prices (model only where no price exists). Under each estimate is the Superbet.ro decimal price for that outcome, with its value at our estimate. Switch the view to compare the model, the market anchor and Superbet's own de-vigged probabilities. Click a column to sort, or a match to open its dossier.

Shading = probability in the selected view.xG = projected goals (home v away) from the final distribution.Rel. = lowest data-reliability rating across the five markets.Market anchor = the de-vigged prices blended into the estimate: Sofascore's odds feed for 75 fixtures, Superbet.ro for 28, none yet for 8.
